Who We Are

ABOUT M2J

M2J Auto Detailing is a Cincinnati-based business founded by three people who share a passion for cars, hard work, and providing quality service.

We believe detailing is about more than making a car look clean. It's about taking pride in our work, being dependable, and building trust with our customers.

As a faith-driven business, we strive to apply Christian values to the way we operate, from the effort we put into each vehicle to the respect we show everyone we serve.
