Questions? We've Got Answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before requesting your M2J detail. Click any question to see the answer.

How much does a detail cost?
Our packages start at $65 for a Basic Exterior Wash, $85 for an Interior Detail, $120 for a Premium Interior Detail, and $150 for a Full Detail. Prices can vary depending on your vehicle's size and condition. We discuss any additional charges with you before work begins.
How do I book an appointment?
Choose your package and complete the booking request form on our website. We'll review your request and contact you to confirm availability and the appointment details.
How long does a detail take?
The time needed depends on the package you select, your vehicle's size, and its condition. We'll give you an estimated completion time when we confirm your appointment.
What is included in a Full Detail?
Our Full Detail includes the Premium Interior Detail and Basic Exterior Wash, with a starting price of $150. That's $35 less than booking the two packages separately. See our service comparison for a full breakdown.
Can you remove stains and pet hair?
Let us know about stains or pet hair in your booking request. We can evaluate the extra cleaning needed and discuss pricing with you. Some stains or embedded pet hair may not be completely removable.
Should I remove my belongings before my appointment?
Yes, please remove personal belongings, valuables, and any important items from the areas being detailed. This helps us clean thoroughly and efficiently.
Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?
Not yet. Submitting the form sends M2J a booking request, but your appointment is only confirmed after we contact you and agree on a date and time.
What areas does M2J serve?
M2J serves the Cincinnati, Ohio area. Contact us to confirm whether your location is within our service area and to discuss where the detailing appointment will take place.

Still have a question? We're happy to help.
